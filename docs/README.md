# Mailopus documentation

For people running Mailopus in their own cloud account. Mailopus runs on Cloudflare today, in your own account.

## Start here

1. [Set up your server](setup.md). Requirements, deploy, DNS, Email Routing, Email Sending and claiming it with a passkey.
2. [Addresses and domains](addresses.md). Adding addresses, `+` aliases, the catch-all route, more domains, more people.

## Using it

- [Sign-in and discovery](sign-in.md). How app.mailopus.net finds your Worker, where passkeys and sessions live, adding a device, recovery.
- [Mail apps](proxy.md). Running the IMAP and SMTP proxy, app passwords, and setting up desktop and phone mail apps.
- [Spam](spam.md). What goes to Junk, blocked senders, remote classifiers, writing your own rules.
- [Plugins](plugins.md). Connecting a Worker that hears about mail as it arrives and leaves, the `gotmail` alert example, and writing your own.
- [Configuration](configuration.md). Every variable, secret and binding, notifications, Swiftly signatures, Gmail import, backups and JMAP for other clients.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). Symptoms and fixes, from deploy to mail apps.
